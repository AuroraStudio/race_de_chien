Retrievers, flushing dogs and water dogs

Group 8 brings together retrievers (Golden and Labrador Retrievers), flushing dogs (cockers, springers) and water dogs. Cheerful, easy to train and very sociable, they are among the best family dogs, as long as they get plenty of exercise.
