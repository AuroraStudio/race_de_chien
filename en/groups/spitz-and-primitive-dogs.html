Spitz and primitive-type dogs

Group 5 includes the spitz breeds (Nordic sled dogs such as the Husky or the Malamute, German Spitz, Shiba, Akita…) and the primitive types that have stayed close to their ancestors, such as the Basenji or the Pharaoh Hound. Independent and often undemonstrative, they need a patient owner.
