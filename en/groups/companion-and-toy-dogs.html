Companion and toy dogs

Companion and toy dogs were bred to live alongside people: bichons, poodles, Cavalier King Charles, Chihuahuas, French Bulldogs… Often small and affectionate, they adapt well to apartment life. Flat-faced breeds cope poorly with heat and exertion.
