Pinschers, schnauzers, molossers and Swiss mountain dogs

Group 2 brings together three families: pinschers and schnauzers, molossers (mastiff types and mountain dogs such as the Newfoundland or the Saint Bernard) and Swiss mountain dogs. Often large and powerful, they were once guard, protection and draft dogs: calm at home, but protective.
