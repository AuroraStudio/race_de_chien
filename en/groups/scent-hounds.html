Scent hounds and blood-tracking dogs

Scent hounds hunt in packs, following the trail of game and giving voice; blood-tracking dogs find wounded game. Beagles, bassets, griffons, Bloodhounds: these are sociable, hardy hunting dogs led by their nose, and they roam easily once they pick up a trail.
