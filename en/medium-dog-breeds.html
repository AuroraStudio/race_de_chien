Medium-sized dogs: every breed from 40 to 60 cm

Between 40 and 60 cm at the withers, medium-sized dogs are the most common compromise: sturdy enough for long walks, compact enough for the car and the home. Shepherds, spaniels, pointers, spitz: temperaments range from very calm to very sporty.
